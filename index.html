📘 Grade 8 Reviewer Cheat Sheet

1. Laboratory Apparatuses (Grade 8)

  • A. Mass / Weight / Volume: Triple beam or digital balance measures mass (g); spring scale measures weight/force (N); graduated cylinder measures liquid volume accurately (read bottom of the meniscus at eye level); beakers & Erlenmeyer flasks hold, mix, or estimate liquids roughly.
  • B. Functions: Funnel pours liquids cleanly into narrow openings or holds filter paper; stirring rod mixes liquids; dropper/pipette transfers small drops; test tube & evaporating dish hold/heat small samples.
  • C. Portable Apparatuses: Graduated cylinder, Erlenmeyer flask, beaker, dropper/pipette, funnel, test tube, stirring rod, and evaporating dish.

2. General Lab Safety (Your Lecture Slides)

  • Protects: You, other lab workers, cleaners, visitors, and your work.
  • Laws & Risk Assessment: Health Safety at Work etc Act 1974 (work safely, don't endanger others, don't misuse safety equipment; penalty up to 2 years prison &/or unlimited fine), Management of H&S at Work Regs 1999, COSHH Regs 2004. Steps: determine hazards & evaluate risks, use relevant data, determine controls, document, agree with supervisor, and apply controls (with specific training).
  • 8 Control Measures (in order of preference): (1) Less risky substance; (2) Safer form (e.g., solution instead of powder); (3) Totally enclose (e.g., glove-box); (4) Partially enclose (e.g., fume cupboard); (5) Good general ventilation; (6) Safe systems of work; (7) Reduce exposure time, increase distance, reduce volumes; (8) PPE (last resort for primary protection).
  • Protecting Yourself, Gloves & Hygiene: No sandals/open shoes; tie long hair back; wear risk-assessment clothing/coats properly; match gloves to substance and remove before using instruments, telephone, or leaving lab. Never eat, drink, smoke, apply cosmetics, touch face/mouth/eyes, or chew pens/pencils; always wash hands before leaving and before eating.
  • Hazards & Symbols: Fire, glassware breakage, sharps, spillages, pressure/gas cylinders, heat & cold extremes, chemical, biological, radiation; symbols include General Warning, Biohazard, Flammable, Explosive, Electrical, High Voltage, Toxic, Ionizing & Non-Ionizing Radiation, Low Temp/Cold, Hot Surface/Burn, UV Light, Oxidizing, Corrosive, Glassware Breakage, Carcinogen/Cancer, and Irritant.

3. DNA Replication & Transcription / Translation

  • DNA Replication: Makes an identical DNA copy before cell division. Strands unzip/separate, and matching bases pair up: A ↔ T and C ↔ G.
  • Transcription (in nucleus): DNA serves as a template to build mRNA (A → U, T → A, C → G, G → C; RNA uses Uracil [U] instead of Thymine).
  • Translation (at ribosome): mRNA is read in 3-base codons, and tRNA brings matching amino acids to build a protein.
